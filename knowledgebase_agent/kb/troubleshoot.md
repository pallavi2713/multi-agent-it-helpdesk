
# VPN Connectivity Troubleshooting

## Issue: VPN is not connecting

### Possible causes
- Incorrect VPN credentials
- Unstable internet connection
- VPN client is outdated
- Firewall blocking VPN traffic
- VPN server is unavailable

### Resolution steps
1. Check whether your internet connection is working.
2. Disconnect the VPN and reconnect.
3. Verify your username and password.
4. Restart the VPN client.
5. Restart your system if the issue persists.
6. Check whether your VPN client has pending updates.
7. If the issue continues, contact the IT network team.

## Issue: VPN connects but internet is unavailable

### Resolution steps
1. Disconnect the VPN.
2. Verify that internet access works without VPN.
3. Reconnect to the VPN.
4. Restart the network adapter.
5. If the issue persists, contact the IT network team.

## Issue: VPN authentication failed

### Possible causes
- Incorrect username or password
- Expired password
- Account locked
- Multi-factor authentication failure

### Resolution steps
1. Verify your username and password.
2. Check whether your password has expired.
3. Complete the MFA verification.
4. If your account is locked, contact IT support for an unlock.
